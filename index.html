How I work

Approach

Metrics before models

Agree on the business metric and the evaluation metric that tracks it before training anything. At 0.17% fraud, AUPRC, not accuracy, is the number that matters.

Monitoring is part of the model

A model ships with its drift checks, alert thresholds and retraining trigger. Evidently, CloudWatch and Prometheus belong in the first pull request, not the last.

Reproducible by default

Versioned data (DVC), tracked experiments and a registry (MLflow), containerized serving and CI on every push, so any result can be rebuilt from the repository.
